The organization has not yet indicated whether it is considering declaring the outbreak a Public Health Emergency of International Concern, its highest level of alert.

Its distinctive hook, built around a sample of Lyn Collins' "Think (About It)," became one of the most recognizable sounds in hip-hop history.

DJ E-Z Rock died in 2014 at age 46. Base continued to perform as a solo act in the years following, keeping the legacy of their collaboration alive through live performances and appearances.

Base's death represents the loss of an artist who helped define the sound of late 1980s hip-hop during a pivotal era when the genre was expanding its mainstream appeal.
