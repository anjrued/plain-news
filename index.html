5:31 PM ET

President Trump approved an emergency declaration for California following a chemical leak in Orange County, Governor Gavin Newsom announced. The leak occurred in Garden Grove and has displaced residents for multiple consecutive nights.

The emergency declaration authorizes the Federal Emergency Management Agency to coordinate disaster relief efforts and provide assistance to save lives, protect property, public health and safety, and reduce the threat of further catastrophe. The declaration allows federal resources to support the state and local response to the chemical incident.

Evacuation zones remain in place around the affected area as officials continue to monitor the situation. Residents in parts of Orange County are waiting for updates on when they might be able to return to their homes. Evacuation maps have been published to help residents determine whether their properties fall within affected zones.

Residents have been displaced for four consecutive nights as authorities work to contain the leak and assess safety conditions. The chemical leak has disrupted normal life for thousands of residents in the Garden Grove area and surrounding communities.

Disneyland issued an operations update in response to the chemical threat in nearby Garden Grove. The theme park's statement addressed how the incident might affect its operations.

Local officials in Orange County continue to provide updates as the situation develops. The emergency declaration enables funding and coordination assistance from federal agencies to help with evacuation, cleanup, and public safety efforts.

Multiple agencies are coordinating response efforts to address the ongoing crisis and ensure public safety in the affected areas. The situation has prompted concerns about environmental impacts and public health risks in the region.

The type of chemical involved in the leak and the cause of the incident have not been detailed in available reports. Authorities are working to contain the breach and assess the full extent of the damage and contamination.

The federal emergency declaration streamlines the process for accessing resources and support needed to respond to the crisis. Such declarations typically enable rapid mobilization of federal personnel and funding to assist state and local authorities with immediate response and recovery operations.

As the situation continues to develop, residents displaced by the chemical leak remain reliant on updates from local authorities about evacuation status and when it may be safe to return to their homes. The scale of the displacement and the duration of the evacuation underscore the severity of the incident and the challenges facing officials working to address the chemical threat.

Oil prices fell sharply following reports that the United States and Iran are moving closer to a peace agreement. Brent crude, the international benchmark for oil, dropped 6% to $97.43 a barrel, marking its lowest level in two weeks as traders reassessed expectations for global energy supplies.

The potential breakthrough in negotiations triggered immediate reactions across global energy markets. President Trump stated that an agreement would include the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz, though he provided limited additional details about the proposed deal. Iran's foreign ministry spokesperson, Esmaeil Baqaei, confirmed that exchanges were "continuing through the Pakistani mediator" without offering specifics on progress.

South Korea's Kospi stock index closed 0.3% higher as investor optimism grew over the possibility of ending the near three-month conflict. However, broader Asian markets showed more caution, with Japan's Nikkei dropping about 1% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng index falling 1%. Shanghai's SSE Composite slipped 0.1%, while European stock markets opened lower, with the Stoxx Europe 600 dropping 0.7%.

Iran possesses significant oil reserves that have been largely isolated from global markets due to international sanctions. A diplomatic breakthrough could allow Iranian oil to enter world markets more freely, potentially increasing global supply and putting downward pressure on prices. Lower oil costs typically reduce input costs for transportation and manufacturing, though the actual economic impact would depend on the final terms of any agreement and how quickly Iranian production could increase.

The price movements reflect trader expectations rather than any confirmed deal. Market analysts noted that negotiations remain in progress and no formal announcement has been made by either government. Energy markets have historically shown significant volatility in response to geopolitical developments in the Middle East.

The potential agreement comes as traders continue weighing the implications of any U.S.-Iran deal against other Middle East developments. Uncertainty about near-term price direction persists given the complexity of the ongoing negotiations.

Any agreement would mark a significant shift in U.S.-Iran relations, which have been strained for years over nuclear program concerns and regional tensions. The timing of the reports coincides with heightened global focus on energy security and supply chain stability, with G7 finance ministers gathering in Paris to discuss the economic impact of Middle East conflicts.

Investors in energy stocks and commodities markets continue monitoring developments closely. The price movements suggest markets are pricing in at least some probability of successful conclusion to the talks, though considerable uncertainty remains about the final outcome and the timing of any potential agreement.

The integration of Gemini, Google's AI model, allows for what the company describes as more interactive and responsive search experiences. Critics have raised concerns about how AI-powered search might affect website traffic and the broader internet ecosystem. The new interface pulls information directly into search results, potentially reducing the need for users to click through to source websites. Industry observers note that the redesign reflects broader changes in how people expect to interact with technology. The traditional model of typing a query and receiving a list of links is being replaced by more conversational and direct answers powered by AI models. Google has not provided specific details about how the new search box will affect advertising revenue or website referral traffic. The company processes billions of searches daily, making even small interface changes significant for publishers, businesses, and users worldwide. The rollout marks a major shift in Google's core product. The search box has been the company's primary interface with users since 1998, and this redesign represents the most significant change to that fundamental interaction in the company's history.

Franco has been away from the Rays since his arrest became public. The 23-year-old signed an 11-year, $182 million contract extension with Tampa Bay in 2021, representing one of the largest deals in franchise history. He made his major league debut in 2021 and quickly became a key player for the team before the legal proceedings began.

Major League Baseball placed Franco on administrative leave when the investigation became public. The league has not yet announced what additional discipline, if any, Franco might face under its policies.

The Rays have not commented on Franco's status with the team following the court decision. According to reports, Franco has yet to contact his organization. The team will need to determine how to proceed with a player under contract through 2032 who now has a criminal finding against him.

The unusual legal outcome combines a guilty finding with avoidance of imprisonment. Franco remains criminally responsible for the offense in the eyes of the court while escaping jail time. The specific terms of the pardon and any conditions attached to it were not detailed in available reports.

This case attracted significant attention given Franco's status as one of baseball's rising stars at the time of his arrest. The combination of his prominence in the sport and the serious nature of the charges kept the matter in the public eye throughout the proceedings.

The conviction of the minor's mother on charges of sexually trafficking her daughter represents a separate criminal resolution in the case. Her 10-year prison sentence reflects the severity of her role in the situation.

As Franco's legal situation concludes, questions remain about his future in professional baseball. MLB's administrative leave continues, and the league must determine whether additional discipline is warranted given the court's ruling. The Rays organization faces decisions about whether to keep Franco on their roster or attempt to move his contract.

The judicial pardon represents an unconventional resolution that holds Franco legally responsible while sparing him from incarceration. His return to baseball, if it occurs, would likely face significant scrutiny from MLB, the Rays organization, and the public.

6:54 PM ET

Disney's "The Mandalorian and Grogu" claimed the top spot at the box office over Memorial Day weekend, but the opening numbers reveal troubling signs for the Star Wars franchise on the big screen. The film earned $102 million domestically and $165 million globally, marking the lowest opening weekend for any Star Wars film since Disney acquired the franchise in 2012.

The performance falls short of even Solo: A Star Wars Story, which Disney previously considered a disappointment. Solo opened on the same Memorial Day weekend in 2018 and took $103 million domestically, translating to $171 million globally. Despite its underperformance, Solo still surpassed the new film's global opening.

The movie reunites audiences with Pedro Pascal's masked warrior character and the creature known as Grogu, or "Baby Yoda," bringing the characters from the Disney+ streaming series to theaters for the first time. The Mandalorian series debuted in 2019 and became one of Disney's most-watched original programs, with over 13 million Grogu toys sold since the show premiered.

Industry analysts note the mixed reception. "The movie is a piece of the story, and it's earning a piece of the business," David Gross told Agence France-Presse, adding that while the numbers are significant, they are "not on the level of the primary Star Wars films."

The film's modest $165 million budget means it could still become profitable, unlike Solo's $275 million production cost. However, the opening signals what analysts describe as continuing Star Wars fatigue. Disney halted theatrical Star Wars releases after 2019's "The Rise of Skywalker," concluding the studio had released too many films too quickly.

Despite mixed critical reviews, Disney executives believe positive audience responses will help the film maintain momentum in coming weeks. The studio is banking on the movie to boost related products, including merchandise, theme park attractions, and Disney+ subscriptions.

The franchise's theatrical struggles contrast sharply with its earlier Disney era, when "The Force Awakens" earned over $2 billion worldwide and "Rogue One" surpassed $1 billion. Even "The Last Jedi," which sparked cultural controversy, collected over $1.3 billion globally.

While Disney has shifted focus to streaming with shows like Andor and The Mandalorian earning critical acclaim, other series including "The Book of Boba Fett," "Obi-Wan Kenobi," and "The Acolyte" failed to gain significant traction.

Disney's next Star Wars theatrical film, "Star Wars: Starfighter" starring Ryan Gosling, is scheduled for May 2027, giving the studio time to assess whether theatrical Star Wars releases can recover their former box office dominance.

Trump administration pauses deportation flights to Congo over Ebola concerns

The pause stems from concerns about exposing Immigration and Customs Enforcement personnel to the virus and the potential risk of bringing the disease closer to the United States. However, the decision has...

May 21, 5:06 PM ET

The Trump administration has temporarily halted the removal of detainees to the Democratic Republic of Congo as an Ebola outbreak continues to spread throughout the country. The pause stems from concerns about exposing Immigration and Customs Enforcement personnel to the virus and the potential risk of bringing the disease closer to the United States.

However, the decision has created complications for those already deported to the region. At least one woman who was moved to Kinshasa, the capital of the DRC, now finds herself in legal limbo after officials refused to return her despite a judge's order for her return, citing the Ebola travel restrictions.

Public health experts have questioned whether the pause will actually help prevent disease spread. The outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo has been ongoing for years, with the virus spreading through direct contact with bodily fluids of infected people or animals. The disease can cause severe illness with high fatality rates in untreated cases.

The move reflects broader concerns about America's public health infrastructure readiness. Recent viral outbreaks have exposed what some observers describe as a trust deficit in the public health system, prompting questions about preparedness for potential threats.

The pause applies specifically to deportation operations to Congo and does not extend to other deportation operations in different regions. ICE has not specified how long the suspension will remain in effect or what conditions would need to be met before flights resume.

The temporary halt occurs amid wider scrutiny of the Trump administration's expanded deportation flight program. A group of 40 House Democrats recently sent a letter to the Federal Aviation Administration expressing grave concerns about what they describe as secretive deportation flights and allegations of mistreatment and inhumane conditions on ICE charter jets.

The lawmakers have demanded transparency regarding the flights, noting that credible reports indicate individuals have been placed on flights without notice to counsel or family members. They cite concerns that flights are being shielded from public tracking systems, making it difficult for families to locate loved ones and for attorneys to intervene.

According to monitoring by human rights groups, ICE flights surged significantly during 2025, with an 84 percent increase from 2024. The lawmakers have requested detailed reports from the FAA regarding all ICE air operations since Trump took office, including flight origin and destination data and passenger numbers.

The administration has previously defended its practices, characterizing claims of hidden deportation flights as categorically false and arguing its detention centers maintain higher standards than most US prisons.

The Congo national soccer team recently cancelled training in Kinshasa due to health restrictions related to the outbreak, though the team still plans to travel to the United States for World Cup preparations.

Instead of the traditional simple text box that has defined Google since its founding, the new interface incorporates AI capabilities that change how queries are processed and results are delivered. Google CEO Sundar Pichai addressed public concerns about artificial intelligence during the announcement. The company is positioning the change as a natural evolution of search technology, though the move represents a fundamental shift in how billions of people access information online. The redesign affects the basic search box that has remained largely unchanged since Google's launch. The integration of Gemini, Google's AI model, allows for what the company describes as more interactive and responsive search experiences. Google has branded the AI agent powering these changes as Gemini Spark. The change comes as Google faces increasing questions about how AI is reshaping the web. Critics have raised concerns about how AI-powered search might affect website traffic and the broader internet ecosystem. The new interface pulls information directly into search results, potentially reducing the need for users to click through to source websites. Industry observers note that the redesign reflects broader changes in how people expect to interact with technology. The traditional model of typing a query and receiving a list of links is being replaced by more conversational and direct answers powered by AI models. Google has not provided specific details about how the new search box will affect advertising revenue or website referral traffic. The company processes billions of searches daily, making even small interface changes significant for publishers, businesses, and users worldwide. The rollout marks a major shift in Google's core product. The search box has been the company's primary interface with users since 1998, and this redesign represents the most significant change to that fundamental interaction in the company's history.

The film earned $102 million domestically and $165 million globally, marking the lowest opening weekend for any Star Wars film since Disney acquired the franchise in 2012.

The performance falls short of even Solo: A Star Wars Story, which Disney previously considered a disappointment. Solo opened on the same Memorial Day weekend in 2018 and took $103 million domestically, translating to $171 million globally. Despite its underperformance, Solo still surpassed the new film's global opening.

The movie reunites audiences with Pedro Pascal's masked warrior character and the creature known as Grogu, or "Baby Yoda," bringing the characters from the Disney+ streaming series to theaters for the first time. The Mandalorian series debuted in 2019 and became one of Disney's most-watched original programs, with over 13 million Grogu toys sold since the show premiered.

Industry analysts note the mixed reception. "The movie is a piece of the story, and it's earning a piece of the business," David Gross told Agence France-Presse, adding that while the numbers are significant, they are "not on the level of the primary Star Wars films."

The film's modest $165 million budget means it could still become profitable, unlike Solo's $275 million production cost. However, the opening signals what analysts describe as continuing Star Wars fatigue. Disney halted theatrical Star Wars releases after 2019's "The Rise of Skywalker," concluding the studio had released too many films too quickly.

Despite mixed critical reviews, Disney executives believe positive audience responses will help the film maintain momentum in coming weeks. The studio is banking on the movie to boost related products, including merchandise, theme park attractions, and Disney+ subscriptions.

The franchise's theatrical struggles contrast sharply with its earlier Disney era, when "The Force Awakens" earned over $2 billion worldwide and "Rogue One" surpassed $1 billion. Even "The Last Jedi," which sparked cultural controversy, collected over $1.3 billion globally.

While Disney has shifted focus to streaming with shows like Andor and The Mandalorian earning critical acclaim, other series including "The Book of Boba Fett," "Obi-Wan Kenobi," and "The Acolyte" failed to gain significant traction.

Disney's next Star Wars theatrical film, "Star Wars: Starfighter" starring Ryan Gosling, is scheduled for May 2027, giving the studio time to assess whether theatrical Star Wars releases can recover their former box office dominance.
