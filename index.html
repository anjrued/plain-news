Yesterday, 4:32 PM ET

Governor Gavin Newsom has requested an emergency declaration from President Trump to support response efforts in Orange County, where a chemical tank leak at an aerospace facility in Garden Grove has forced approximately 50,000 residents to evacuate their homes.

The incident began Thursday afternoon when a leak was reported at GKN Aerospace, which manufactures parts for commercial and military aircraft. The storage tank contains 6,000 to 7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate, a highly flammable chemical used to produce plastic and resin.

EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin warned on Sunday that the overheated chemical tank "will fail," though he acknowledged different scenarios exist for what that failure could mean. "The most catastrophic scenario" would be an explosion that triggers other tanks to explode, Zeldin told CNN, explaining the scale of the evacuation. However, officials have indicated the most likely scenario involves a "low-volume release" of chemicals.

Emergency crews have been spraying water on the tank to keep temperatures below 85 degrees Fahrenheit and prevent an explosion. On Friday, officials added a neutralizing agent to a nearby tank to further limit explosion risks.

A development late Saturday night offered some relief. Firefighters discovered a potential crack in the tank, which could be relieving internal pressure and making an explosion less likely. Purdue University engineering professor Andrew Whelton explained the benefit using a simple analogy: "Think of a soda can. If you leave it in a hot car it can explode. But if you put a hole in the can, the product is released and the can itself doesn't explode."

However, experts urged caution. A Texas A&M chemical engineering department head noted that while cooling is reducing the reaction rate, the risk of an explosive release has not been eliminated. The crack could eventually allow authorities to gradually drain the chemicals from the tank.

Garden Grove Mayor Stephanie Klopfenstein urged residents to take evacuation orders seriously, calling it "a very dangerous situation." She later reported receiving "a somewhat positive briefing" from county fire officials. The Orange County Fire Authority stated they are currently focused on measuring pressure within the tank.

Air quality monitoring has detected no pollutants in the area. Officials said methyl methacrylate is easy to smell and residents may notice it over a large area without being harmed. However, exposure can cause serious respiratory problems, neurological issues, and irritation to skin, eyes, and throat.

At least two lawsuits have been filed against GKN Aerospace by Garden Grove residents seeking damages for evacuation orders, shelter-in-place directives, exposure concerns, and property damage.

The nearby Disneyland theme parks in Anaheim were not under evacuation orders, though park officials said they were monitoring the incident and supporting affected employees.

Government headquarters also suffered damage with windows blown out, though Prime Minister Yulia Svyrydenko said no one was injured there.

The EU's foreign policy chief called the attacks "abhorrent acts of terror" and described the Oreshnik deployment as "political scare tactic and reckless nuclear brinkmanship."

Google announced a redesign of its search box, the first major change to the feature in 25 years. The company revealed the update at its I/O conference, introducing AI-powered suggestions and prompts directly into the search interface.

The new search box integrates Google's Gemini AI technology to offer conversational prompts and suggestions before users finish typing. The company framed the change as part of what it called the "agentic Gemini era," emphasizing AI-driven interactions over traditional keyword searches.

The redesign arrives as Google faces competitive pressure from AI-powered search alternatives. The company has been working to incorporate generative AI into its core search product, including AI Overviews that summarize results at the top of search pages.

Google also launched a product called Spark at the same conference. The timing of the search box redesign reflects broader industry competition in AI-powered information retrieval, with multiple companies racing to reimagine how users find and interact with information online.

The changes represent a fundamental shift in how Google presents its primary product. The search box has remained largely unchanged since the company's founding, serving as the entry point for billions of daily searches. The new design aims to make search more conversational and anticipatory rather than reactive.

Google described the update as reinventing search before competitors could replace it with alternative AI tools. The company has invested heavily in AI capabilities following the emergence of chatbot interfaces that offer direct answers rather than lists of links.

The redesigned interface began rolling out following the I/O announcement. Users will see AI-generated prompts and suggestions that aim to help refine queries or explore related topics. The change marks one of the most visible transformations in Google's history, altering the look and function of its most recognizable feature.

The search box redesign is part of a broader strategy to integrate AI throughout Google's products while maintaining the company's dominance in online search, which remains its primary revenue source through advertising.

The NASCAR community gathered at Charlotte Motor Speedway on Sunday to honor Kyle Busch before the Coca-Cola 600 race, one week after the two-time Cup Series champion died at age 41.

Busch's family, Richard Childress Racing and NASCAR confirmed that he died from severe pneumonia and sepsis. The Las Vegas native had been hospitalized earlier in the week and was unable to compete in the scheduled race.

The emotional pre-race ceremony brought together Busch's wife Samantha, their children Brexton and Lennix, his parents and his brother, along with thousands of fans at the Charlotte track. The tribute marked one of NASCAR's most somber moments as the sport mourned the loss of one of its greatest drivers.

"A future Hall of Famer, Kyle was a rare talent, one who comes along once in a generation," NASCAR, the Busch family and Richard Childress Racing said in a joint statement. "He was fierce, he was passionate, he was immensely skilled and he cared deeply about the sport and fans."

Busch's health struggles began during a Cup Series race on May 10 at Watkins Glen, where he had been battling a sinus cold. The intense G-forces and elevation changes at the upstate New York road course exacerbated his illness, but he continued racing and finished eighth. He won a truck series race at Dover on Friday, six days before his death.

The veteran driver's career spanned more than two decades. He won 232 races across NASCAR's three national series, more than any driver in history. Busch captured Cup Series championships in 2015 and 2019 and also won titles in both the Xfinity Series and Truck Series, making him one of the few drivers to win championships across all three NASCAR national series.

Busch started his Cup Series career in 2005 as a rookie of the year and quickly established himself as one of racing's most competitive and polarizing figures. Known by nicknames "Rowdy" and "Wild Thing," he became famous for his post-race fights, feuds with other drivers and outspoken personality. His competitive spirit and sharp wit created a devoted fan base known as "Rowdy Nation."

The Coca-Cola 600 proceeded after the ceremony, with Daniel Suárez ultimately winning the race. Suárez, who had driven for Kyle Busch Motorsports in the truck series years earlier, dedicated his victory to his late friend.

"This one is for him," Suárez said after the race, which marked his third career Cup Series victory and ended an 82-race winless streak.

The race was part of NASCAR's traditional Memorial Day weekend programming at Charlotte Motor Speedway, where Busch competed for over two decades.

However, Solo went on to earn $171 million globally in its opening weekend, compared to The Mandalorian and Grogu's $165 million. Despite this, industry analysts suggest the new film could still prove successful given its significantly lower budget of $165 million compared to Solo's $275 million production cost.

"The movie is a piece of the story, and it's earning a piece of the business," industry analyst David Gross told Agence France-Presse, noting that spin-offs typically perform differently than primary Star Wars installments.

The film continues the story of Disney's popular streaming series that debuted in 2019. This marks the first Star Wars theatrical release since The Rise of Skywalker in 2019, following Disney's decision to pause cinema releases after concluding they had released too many films in too short a time.

The opening reflects what some observers characterize as continuing Star Wars fatigue. While the streaming series has found success, Disney's recent Star Wars television shows have produced mixed results. The Mandalorian remains the most-watched original series on Disney Plus, and merchandise tied to Grogu has sold over 13 million units since the show debuted.

Disney executives believe the film will maintain momentum in coming weekends, supported by positive audience scores despite receiving mixed critical reviews. The studio is positioning the film as a test case for future theatrical Star Wars projects. Beyond box office returns, Disney is hoping the release will drive viewers toward other company products, including merchandise, theme parks, and the Disney Plus streaming platform.

The Star Wars franchise has generated over $10 billion in global ticket sales since its 1977 debut. Disney acquired the franchise from George Lucas in 2012 and has since shifted significant Star Wars production to its streaming service. While shows like Andor have garnered critical acclaim and large audiences, others including The Book of Boba Fett, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and The Acolyte have struggled to achieve comparable success.

Disney has not announced specific box office targets for determining success. The company's next theatrical Star Wars release, Starfighter starring Ryan Gosling, is scheduled for May 2027.

May 21, 10:12 AM ET

The Colorado Democratic Party has formally censured Governor Jared Polis following his decision to commute the prison sentence of Tina Peters, a former county clerk whose case has become central to Republican efforts to promote false claims about the 2020 election.

Polis reduced Peters's nearly nine-year sentence to approximately four and a half years, allowing her release on parole in June. In his clemency letter, the governor described the original sentence as "extremely unusual and lengthy for a first time offender who committed non-violent crimes."

Peters, the former clerk in Mesa County in western Colorado, was convicted in 2024 of four felonies and three misdemeanors. In 2021, she allowed an unauthorized person to access the county's voting equipment, copy it, and attend a sensitive upgrade of the county's voting software. The individual she permitted access, Conan Hayes, is affiliated with MyPillow CEO Mike Lindell, a prominent figure in spreading false claims about the 2020 election. Sensitive information from the voting equipment was later published online by right-wing personalities.

Colorado Secretary of State Jena Griswold, a Democrat, strongly criticized the decision. She said the clemency "validates Trump's basest impulses and emboldens this lawless president" and warned it would "embolden the election denial movement across the country."

"What it actually is is special treatment," Griswold stated, noting that Peters should have waited for resentencing through the courts as directed by the Court of Appeals.

Matt Crane, executive director of the Colorado Clerks Association, called the decision deeply troubling. "We're starting to hear from election officials across the country that this signals that it's open season on our elections and election officials," he said.

The judge who originally sentenced Peters rejected any sympathy for her case. "You are no hero," Judge Matthew Barrett told her during sentencing in 2024. "You're a charlatan who used, and is still using, your prior position in office to peddle a snake oil that's been proven to be junk time and time again."

Polis had previously signaled his leanings by comparing Peters's case to that of former state senator Sonya Jaquez Lewis, who received probation and community service for submitting forged letters to the legislature. Both face overlapping felony counts related to attempting to influence a public official. However, Griswold disputed the comparison, stating the cases were fundamentally different.

"Peters organized the breach of the election equipment, broke the public trust and attacked the very foundations of our democratic process," Griswold said. "Her actions are still being used to try to undermine the 2026 election."

The clemency decision marks a significant rift between Polis and his own party, highlighting ongoing tensions within Democratic ranks over how to respond to election-related cases tied to false fraud narratives.
